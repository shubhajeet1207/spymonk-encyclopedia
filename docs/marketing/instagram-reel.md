## Creative direction

**Concept:** Research that shows its work.

An expressive fictional adult tech presenter introduces the product, with layered interface cards and an animated research path. Match the app palette: ink purple `#201a3a`, cream `#fff4e7`, coral `#f05b43`, lavender `#ebc8d7`.

Use a strong first-frame hook, smooth spring motion, dimensional parallax, match cuts, and deliberate beat accents. Keep the presenter visible in the opening, picture-in-picture during the explanation, and the close. Use an unobtrusive “AI presenter” label.

## Spoken script

> Too many tabs? Meet SpyMonk. Ask a question or upload a PDF. Search saved knowledge, compare web sources, and see the citations. Follow every step. Research that shows its work.

Pronounce the brand “Spy Monk.” Deliver naturally and energetically; the script is 30 words. Music should remain below the voice.
